REST & INJURY GUIDE 🧊

Plans build mileage on purpose — your body gets final say.

😴

Rest isn't optional

Running breaks muscles down — rest rebuilds them stronger. Skipping rest doesn't speed progress, it removes recovery.

If plan says easy, keep it easy: slow jog or full rest, not sneaky pace work.

🦵

Common aches

Shin splints — dull ache front lower leg, usually too much too soon.

Runner's knee — soreness around kneecap, often worn shoes.

IT band — sharp outside knee, mileage jumped fast.

🧊

R.I.C.E. basics

For minor niggles: Rest, Ice 15–20 min, Compress gently, Elevate above heart.

Sensible first move while deciding if it needs more than time.

🚩

Stop & get checked

Pain changing how you walk, swelling that stays, pain worsening mid-run, same spot flaring repeatedly — see a doctor/physio, don't push through.

General info, not medical advice. If something feels wrong, see a professional who can examine you.
